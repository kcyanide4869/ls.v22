    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers to common library questions.

    How long can I borrow a book?

    Borrowing periods depend on the library's current lending policy. Check the due date shown in Books To Return.

    Why is my borrow request still pending?

    Borrow requests remain pending until a librarian reviews and approves them.

    How do I return a borrowed book?

    Open Books To Return, choose Request Return, and bring the book to the library for approval.
